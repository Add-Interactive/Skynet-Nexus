🎯 FIELD MISSIONS

Every mission works the same way — the scientist's way: Predict first, then Scan, then Explain what the data showed. Teachers and guardians: any mission can be assigned as homework or a family challenge — just name the mission!

📐 Mission 1 · The Pendulum Test (Gus)

Can your phone measure gravity?

  1. Predict: a longer pendulum swings slower or faster?
  2. Scan: hold your phone gently, swing it like a pendulum, run Motion Lab for 10 swings.
  3. Explain: count the graph's waves — time ÷ swings = your period. Did it match your prediction?

🔊 Mission 2 · Sound Map of Your World (Cadence)

Where is your world loudest — and quietest?

  1. Predict: rank 4 places from quietest to loudest before you measure.
  2. Scan: run Sound Lab in each place for 10 seconds and log the peak.
  3. Explain: compare your logs with your prediction. Any surprises?

🧭 Mission 3 · The North Hunt (Rowan)

Can you navigate by instrument alone?

  1. Predict: point at where you think north is. No peeking!
  2. Scan: start the Geo Compass and check your heading.
  3. Explain: how far off were you, in degrees? Log your true-north find.

🔒 FOR GUARDIANS & TEACHERS

The Field Kit uses only the sensors a learner taps “Start” on, explains each permission in plain language first, and stores scan logs in the browser on this device. It never records audio, never uploads photos, and never tracks location — the compass shows a heading, not a position. Phone sensors are teaching-grade, not lab-grade: missions are built around comparisons and patterns, and learning that every instrument has error is part of the science.
