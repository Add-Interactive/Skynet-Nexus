🛡️ Protected by NEXUS SHIELD.

Skynet Nexus is built for kids — so it's defended like it matters. Our Security Team patrols the site every hour, around the clock: watching the site itself, hunting for bad actors, and verifying our defenses. Here's what that means for your family.

Meet the Security Team

Three specialists, on patrol 24/7. When something needs a human, it goes straight to our team — never into a void.

🛰️

Sentinel — Site Health

Watches the site itself: is it up, is it fast, are logins and APIs responding, are our security certificates valid. If the site ever struggles, Sentinel is the first to know — usually before you are.

🎯

Hunter — Threat Tracker

Watches for the bad guys: probing, break-in attempts, bots hammering logins, scanners looking for weaknesses. Hunter profiles attackers, and our defenses block them automatically.

🛡️

Guardian — Defense Check

Inspects our armor: security headers, file integrity (has anything been tampered with?), and the blocklist. If a single monitored file changes unexpectedly, Guardian raises the alarm.

What that means for your kids

Kid-safe by design. Every article passes a content guardrail — no violence, no politics, no drama for drama's sake. If we can't write about it in a way that respects young readers, we don't run it.

Real names never go public. In comments and public spaces, kids appear as "a young reader" with their avatar — never their first name.

Parents approve first. A kid's comment stays hidden until a parent approves it. Posts pass a profanity screen and rate limits, and parents can wipe their family's data at any time.

Invite-only social. Chats and friend features unlock through family or teacher invites — never open to strangers. Public access is off by default.

Accounts locked down. Passwords are hashed, admins use two-factor authentication, and kid accounts can only ever touch their own data.

No ads tracking your kids. No ad trackers, no data brokers. Ever. Our privacy practices are published in plain language on the Privacy page.

What that means for teachers & schools

Classroom oversight. Teachers see their own students' activity — never anyone else's. Assignments, reading progress, and moderation queues are all visible from the teacher dashboard.

Defense in depth. Automated IP blocking, rate limiting, and threat profiling run constantly; our team reviews what the automation catches. Suspicious patterns get escalated to a human the same hour.

Transparency. We publish what we protect: security updates appear on What's New, and our kid-data practices were reviewed against the FTC's COPPA rule — read the details.

See it in action

Admins can watch the team work live in the site's Security command center — real patrol reports, real threat data, updated hourly. And if you ever spot something that worries you, tell us. A real person reads every message.
