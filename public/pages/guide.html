📖 How to use Skynet Nexus

Skynet Nexus is a news platform for the whole family — parents, kids, and teachers. Not doomscrolling: every story is built for reading together, understanding it, and talking about it. Here's how each of you uses it.

📰 Read→ 🧠 Understand→ 💬 Discuss→ 🏆 Earn
👨‍👩‍👧 Parents 🧒 Kids 🏫 Teachers

👨‍👩‍👧 Parents

You run the household newsroom. Set up profiles, co-read, and watch the XP roll in.

1Set up your family

Sign up as a Parent, then add a kid profile for each child from your profile page — name, age, avatar, and their favorite correspondent art style: Humans, Animals, or Skynet Agents. The whole site re-skins to their pick.

2Co-read with the guided journey

Every article walks you through four steps: the story, the Kid Take (rewritten at an age-8 reading level), Talk Together (discussion questions), and Words to Know (glossary). Read them in order, together — that's the core loop.

3Listen together

Every article has a 🔊 Listen button with studio-quality narration — perfect for car rides, bedtime, or kids who learn by hearing. Pick from 4 voices (Heart, Amy, Ryan, Danny); your choice is remembered across articles.

4Tap the XP buttons together

Each step has an XP button (10 / 5 / 15 / 5 XP). Tap them as you finish — your kid levels up from Rookie Reader to Editor-in-Chief, and you earn grown-up XP for co-reading (+10 per talk-together).

5Track progress, not screen time

Your profile page shows every kid's level, XP bar, reading streak, and badge collection (20 badges: milestones, streaks, channel explorers). It's proof of reading, not minutes stared.

6Make the discussion the point

The Talk Together questions are the heart of the platform. Fifteen XP says it all: the conversation is worth more than the click. Three editions drop daily (7:15 AM, 2:15 PM, 6:15 PM ET) so there's always something fresh to talk about.

7Vote in the Weekend Lab poll

Every Thursday evening, pick which of the week's biggest stories become Saturday's hands-on STEM activities. The Weekend Lab is built around what families vote for — your picks shape the weekend.

8Build your family tree

Create a family from your dashboard, share your invite code with grandparents, aunts, uncles and cousins — only true family with the code can join. Everyone gets a private family chat.

9Control what each kid sees

Per-kid Controls: Listen on/off, exactly which of the 13 channels they see, discussions on/off, and public chat access (off by default — kids stay in family & classroom only). See their full comment and question history with links to join the thread.

10Accessibility for every kid

Declare blindness or low vision in your kid's profile to unlock the full suite: spoken audio guide, voice commands ("read this", "next story"), gesture navigation, large text and high contrast.

11Message teachers privately

Open Teacher messages on your dashboard to start a private 1:1 with any of your kids' teachers — conference requests, progress questions, quick check-ins. Each conversation gets its own private chat space.

12Read together, guided

Tap the 📖 button on any article to start a Read Together session: you see discussion prompts inline, your kid gets the Kid Take front and center, and checkmarks track your progress through the story together.

13Cheer on the whole family

The Family feed shows achievements across your tree — send one-tap cheers to grandkids, nieces, nephews. Kids see cheers on their dashboard.

14Get the weekly email

A digest of your crew's week — XP, streaks, and a dinner-table conversation starter. Toggle it in settings.

🧒 Kids

You're the Junior Correspondent. Read stories, earn XP, climb the newsroom ranks.

1Make your space yours

Open My Space (tap your card in the family dashboard): pick from 100 avatars, upload a banner, write your quote, and choose your interests. It's your cinematic home base — achievements, quiz scores, streaks, family, classrooms, all in one animated dashboard.

2Share your creations

Built something cool? Submit it from your dashboard — your parent approves, and it goes live in the Creations Gallery where other families can ❤️ it.

3Save stories to shelves

Tap 🔖 on any article to save it. Your shelves live on your dashboard — "want to read" and "favorites."

4Read the day's editions

New front pages drop at 7:15 AM, 2:15 PM, and 6:15 PM Eastern. Explore 13 channels — Space, Robotics, Gaming, Music, AI, and more — each with its own correspondent.

5Listen to stories

Tap 🔊 Listen on any article to hear it read aloud in a real voice — great for long stories or listening together. Try all 4 voices and pick your favorite.

6Finish steps, earn XP

Tap the XP buttons as you complete each reading step. Rack up XP to climb: Rookie Reader → Cub Reporter → Beat Reporter → Correspondent → Senior Correspondent → Editor → Editor-in-Chief.

7Collect badges & streaks

Earn 20 badges — finish your first story, read 3 days in a row (then 7, then 30!), become an explorer of every channel. Your progress card on the profile page shows it all.

8Chat safely

Talk with your family and classmates in private chats — open Chats to see your spaces. Want to chat with a friend? Share friend codes in real life — both parents have to approve before you can talk. The public square stays hidden unless your parent allows it. Be kind, and flag anything weird with 🚩.

9Take the Daily Quiz

Every evening, 5 questions from the day's stories appear on the Quiz page. One try per day, 5 XP per correct answer — see how you rank on the leaderboard.

10Ask the Correspondent

Curious about something in a story? Ask the correspondent who wrote it — right from the article page. They answer in kid-friendly language, and you can read other kids' questions too.

🏫 Teachers

Turn the news into a classroom activity — with live reading data on every student.

1Create your teacher account

Sign up as a Teacher, then create a student profile for each student — same system parents use for kids, no separate logins to manage.

2Build your classroom

Open the Classroom page (linked from your profile), create a class like "Ms. Rivera's 4th Grade," and add your students. One teacher can run multiple classes.

3Track reading live

The progress dashboard shows every student's XP, level, reading streak, stories finished, and badge count — sorted by XP. You'll know who's reading and who needs a nudge, without a single quiz to grade.

4Start article discussions

Pick an article (paste its URL) or just a topic, and open a threaded discussion for the class. Students reply, everyone sees everything classroom-style, and you moderate. You earn +20 XP for hosting.

5Make news a habit

Use case ideas: morning edition warm-up (read the 7:15 AM drop together), discussion prompts (one thread per week per class), channel challenges (which team earns the most explorer badges this month?). The editions do the content work — you do the teaching.

6Assign articles & get drop alerts

Assign specific stories to your class from the Assignments page and track who's finished. Turn on browser notifications to get pinged the moment each edition drops — 7:15 AM, 2:15 PM, 6:15 PM ET.

7Invite parents (you can't add kids)

Enter a parent's email — they create the account and link their child to your class. You never touch kid accounts directly. Parents stay in control; you get a live roster.

8Build your public profile

Add your photo, bio, teaching quote, degrees and awards. Families preview your profile before joining — it's your professional showcase.

9Set class defaults

Per-class controls: Listen on/off and which channels students see. Parents can further restrict per kid — your settings are the classroom baseline.

10Broadcast announcements

Post announcements from your dashboard — field trips, quiz reminders, permission slips. Pin the important ones to the top. Parents see them in Class news.

11Message parents 1:1

Private threads with each family about their child — progress notes, conference requests, quick questions. Find them under Parent messages; each gets a private chat space.
